What is Threadnote?

Threadnote is a fictional feedback workspace, created as an interactive design example. The sample shows how conversations can connect to themes and product decisions.

Can I try it without an account?

Yes. Open the sample and follow the three steps. There is nothing to sign up for and no payment information to enter.

Is this real customer data?

No. All people, messages, counts and decisions in this concept are fictional sample data.

Does the sample connect my tools?

No. Research calls, support notes and team interviews illustrate source context. The sample does not access any external account.

Can I change the sample?

You can filter the notes, inspect the theme, read the decision and open its source evidence. Your selections reset when you reload the page.

Can I pause the animation?

Yes. Use Pause motion below the feature cards. If your device requests reduced motion, the page starts with a still composition.

Under consideration

Search beyond the project name

Explore searching by client and project description, so people can find work using the details they remember.

Why this direction?

The notes point to a recall problem, not a request for more filters. Testing a wider search is a starting hypothesis, not a validated solution.

What still needs testing

Can people find an older project by client or description? The sample does not establish that this change will work.
