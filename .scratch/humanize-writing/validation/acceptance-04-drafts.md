## h11-en-new-rules.md

    The scheduler is at the center of our platform. It is fast and simple.

Almost all of this draft was framing with nothing behind it, so very little survives. Here is what I removed and why:

- "Nestled at the heart of" and "serves as a testament to our commitment to reliability" were brochure wording plus an inflated tail. I kept only where the scheduler sits.
- I cut "Experts argue that this approach is the future" because it names no source. If you have one, name it. If it's your own view, say it as yours. I didn't make that call for you.
- I cut "Despite facing challenges, the team continues to thrive" because it's a stock formula. If there were real problems, describe them: when they happened, how bad they were, and what you did.
- The two emoji bullets just repeated their bold headers ("Speed: ... is fast"), so I merged them into one plain sentence. The draft gives no numbers for speed or simplicity. Adding one, like a latency or a line count, would do more than any adjective.
- "I hope this helps! Let me know..." was leftover chatbot text, so I deleted it.
