# Review independently designed cases as a batch

Use when producing or extending several independently designed products or brands. A successful example establishes a quality level; carry its craft forward without turning its composition into the next industry template.

## Before production

For each case, record the user job, content object, successful outcome, first-screen hierarchy, representative operation and closest existing peer. Explain which content or task decision changes the structure. Select one coherent direction within the current decision grant; do not create extra alternatives solely to fill a batch.

Build and inspect the representative screen before extending that case. Compare its rendered desktop and mobile views with the closest peer early enough to change its concept. After completing the flow, compare a downstream screen or section and the main operation as well. Keep relevant existing evidence when its source is unchanged.

## Judge the comparison

Inspect reading order, density, composition, content rhythm, headline construction, controls and state transitions. If swapping brand names, photographs and colors would leave the same story and flow intact, identify whether a shared task actually warrants that structure. When it does not, revise the information hierarchy or interaction before producing more pages; cosmetic swaps do not resolve structural repetition.

Familiar controls and consistent layouts within one product or shared design system can be the right choice. State their task or system reason rather than forcing novelty. Do not use fixed counts of differing axes, media, motion, CSS files or passing automated checks as proof of design distinction.

## At integration

Compare the whole batch and its nearest existing peers at the actual presentation sizes. Save the compared screens and a short observation naming the content-driven differences, any repetition retained with its reason, and corrections made. Include detail screens and representative operations so distinct heroes cannot hide a repeated body or workflow. Keep this judgment separate from mechanical checks and human acceptance, using the evidence record in artifacts.md.
